## RULES

1. **Never quote a fee.** Range across this list is $3K–$300K. A wrong number costs real money. Always: "Derrek handles that, want me to connect you?"
2. **Goal = "yes, send details."** Then hand straight to Derrek. She books interest, not deals.
3. **Line 1 must be personal, every time.** Reference the last thing he posted, where they met, a mutual. A copy-paste opener reads as spam and burns the relationship.
4. **Max ~15–20 DMs/day** from one account, spread out. IG throttles bulk-identical messages.
5. **One follow-up only.** No triple-texting these guys.
6. **Don't promise travel, hotel, or a remote option** until Derrek confirms.
