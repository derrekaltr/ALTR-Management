# Rubric 01 — Sexually Suggestive / Adult-Adjacent Content

The most common client misconception: "there's no nudity, so it's fine." Instagram's
recommendation classifier targets **sexual framing**, not exposure. A fully clothed
video can be non-recommendable; a bikini photo can be fine. Audit for framing.

## What the classifier keys on (audit checklist)

### Visual signals — per post/reel
- Camera framing that centers or lingers on breasts, butt, or crotch (the single
  strongest signal; includes zoom-ins, slow pans, "check the fit" spins shot from behind)
- Implied nudity: hands/objects/censor bars/emoji covering otherwise-nude body,
  "topless but covered" poses, open-shirt framing
- Wet, see-through, or lifted clothing as the focal point of the shot
- Sexualized dance content where the edit repeatedly cuts to body-part close-ups
  (dance itself is fine; the edit pattern is what flags)
- Bedroom/shower/mirror settings combined with lingerie or underwear as the default
  content format (setting + wardrobe + frequency compounds)
- Thumbnail choice: a suggestive cover frame flags the reel even if the video is tame

### Text/audio signals
- Captions or overlays with sexual innuendo, "spicy," "18+," "you know where to find
  the rest," "link for the good stuff"
- Sexual-context emoji clusters (🍑🍆💦🥵) in captions, comments the creator posts, or bio
- Audio tracks whose lyrics are explicit AND the visual is body-focused (either alone
  is usually survivable; together they compound)

### Account-level signals (often the real problem)
- **Bio or link-in-bio routing to adult platforms.** Instagram resolves the link and
  checks ONE HOP past aggregators: a Linktree/Beacons page containing an OnlyFans/Fansly
  link makes the IG account non-recommendable, and explicit "subscribe to my page"
  CTAs cross into Sexual Solicitation (Layer 1, strikeable).
- Username/display-name terms: "spicy," "afterdark," "xxx," "free[anything]18"
- Highlights with suggestive covers or names — highlights are scored like content
- A grid where >~30% of recent posts individually score MEDIUM+ on the visual signals
  above → assume account-level ineligibility is active or imminent

## Severity mapping
- Explicit solicitation, OF-style monetization CTAs, implied-nudity content → **CRITICAL**
- Body-part-focused framing as the recurring format; adult link one hop away → **HIGH**
- Occasional suggestive post in an otherwise varied grid; suggestive thumbnails → **MEDIUM**
- Emoji/wording hygiene, risky audio pairings → **LOW**

## Fix tiers (give the client all applicable options per finding)

**Tier 1 — Adjust (keep the post/format):**
- Recut so the body is context, not subject: wider framing, fewer close-up inserts,
  faces and activity in frame. Fitness, fashion, and swim content survives fine when
  the shot is about the activity or the garment, not the anatomy.
- Swap the reel cover frame for a neutral one (this alone recovers some reels).
- Strip innuendo from caption/overlays; rewrite around the actual topic (the workout,
  the outfit source, the routine).
- Same wardrobe, different set: gym/beach/street context reads completely differently
  from bedroom/shower.

**Tier 2 — Rework (change the format):**
- Move from "look at me" content to "watch me do / learn from me" content: tutorials,
  transformations, day-in-the-life, styling breakdowns. Keeps the aesthetic, changes
  the subject of the frame.
- If the business is adult-platform subscriptions: Instagram cannot be the sales page.
  The compliant model is a genuinely SFW Instagram brand (fitness, lifestyle, comedy)
  whose audience finds the paid platform through off-Instagram channels (X, Reddit,
  their own website/newsletter). Any on-IG breadcrumb to the adult platform re-flags.
- Remove adult links from bio AND from the link aggregator page that the bio points to.

**Tier 3 — Delete/archive:**
- Implied-nudity posts, solicitation captions, anything CRITICAL: archive now — these
  are strike inventory sitting on the grid, and old posts get re-reviewed when the
  account is under evaluation.
- If account-level ineligibility is suspected: archive everything HIGH+, then 4–6 weeks
  of clean posting before expecting non-follower reach to return. Check Account Status
  weekly and appeal any listed items that were actually fixed.
