# Rubric 04 — General Reach-Killers (check on every audit, any niche)

These rarely cause the client's crisis, but they compound with niche-specific flags
and slow recovery. Sweep all of them in every audit.

## Bio & profile
- Link-in-bio chain: resolve it and check ONE hop. Aggregator pages containing adult
  platforms, peptide vendors, gambling, or crypto links flag the IG account even if
  the IG content is clean.
- Bio keywords: "18+", "spicy", "backup account", "check my Telegram", compound names,
  price lists. Telegram/Snapchat handles in bio are a gray-market classifier signal.
- "Backup/second account" framing signals prior enforcement and links account
  reputations — remove it.
- Category setting: wrong or missing business category limits some distribution
  surfaces; also check the account is NOT unnecessarily flagged 18+ in its own settings.

## Content hygiene (Layer 2 demotions)
- **Watermarks**: visible TikTok (or other-platform) watermarks are explicitly demoted
  in Reels ranking. Re-export clean or use the original file.
- **Unoriginal content**: reposted/aggregated clips without meaningful edits are
  deprioritized and can make the account ineligible for recommendation on originality
  grounds alone.
- **Engagement bait**: "comment X and I'll DM you", "tag 3 friends", "follow to see
  part 2" — classified and demoted; the comment-to-DM automation pattern is under
  active enforcement pressure.
- **Watchbait**: withheld-payoff captions ("wait for it…", "you won't believe") — demoted.
- Hashtag hygiene: banned/restricted hashtags on old posts still count; spammy 30-tag
  blocks read as spam. 3–8 relevant tags.
- Recycled AI voiceover/slideshow spam formats — originality demotion.
- Giveaways requiring follows/shares of third parties — bait classification.

## Behavioral (account activity, not content)
- Follow/unfollow cycles, mass-DM outreach, engagement pods, automation tools with
  session access — all classify as inauthentic behavior; also a common cause of
  "random" action blocks. Ask the client what tools have their login. Revoke
  everything unnecessary in Settings → Website permissions / Apps.
- Posting bursts after long silence, or identical captions across many posts.
- Buying followers/likes ever, on this account — permanent audience-quality drag;
  the fake-follower ratio also suppresses distribution (low engagement-per-reach).

## Recovery mechanics (include in every report's action plan)
1. **Account Status first**: Settings → Account Status. Screenshot both "Removed
   content" and "What can't be recommended." Appeal anything already remediated —
   successful appeals remove strikes; unappealed items age out slower.
2. Archive (don't delete) flagged/risky back-catalog — archiving removes it from
   scoring while preserving it if a policy read turns out wrong.
3. Expect a lag: reach recovery after cleanup typically shows in 2–6 weeks of
   consistent clean posting, not days. Set client expectations or they'll churn back
   to old content at week 1.
4. During recovery: no aggressive growth tactics, no automation, steady posting
   cadence (3–5x/week), reply to comments manually. Boring on purpose.
5. Re-audit at 30 days: confirm Account Status is clean and non-follower reach
   (Insights → Reach → non-followers %) is trending up.
