# Rubric 05 — Cross-Platform: YouTube & TikTok
(Grounded Oct 2026 via web verification. Audit any platform that appears in the
client's bio links or landing page; score it against its own system, not Meta's.)

## YouTube

### Enforcement layers
- **Strikes:** 1 warning, then 3 Community Guidelines strikes in 90 days =
  channel termination. A single active strike blocks YouTube Partner Program
  eligibility. Channel deletions are the cross-platform event we keep finding in
  Instagram audits ("old channel got deleted") — always ask for the termination
  email.
- **Age-restriction:** the video exists but is blocked for signed-out and
  under-18 viewers, and is excluded from most recommendation surfaces — the
  YouTube equivalent of reach death.
- **Limited ads ("yellow icon"):** advertiser-friendly guidelines are a separate,
  stricter layer; content can be fully compliant and still demonetized.
- **Borderline demotion:** recommendation suppression for content that brushes
  policy without violating it (the least visible layer).
- **Monetization gates (2026):** YPP = 1K subs + 4K watch hours (rising to 8K for
  new applicants Feb 2027) or 10M Shorts views/90d; no active strikes; 6 months
  of inactivity can forfeit monetization.

### Gambling — YouTube is now the strictest major platform (Mar 2025 policy,
### expanded Nov 2025)
- Creators may NOT direct viewers to any gambling site/app/service not certified
  by Google — **spoken promos, embedded links, logos, screenshots, and even
  casual references** to unlicensed operators all count.
- Online-casino content (excluding sports betting and in-person gambling) is
  age-restricted platform-wide.
- Nov 2025 expansion covers digital-goods gambling: skins, cosmetics, NFT
  wager sites.
- Practical read for our clients: casino-stream VODs and "big win" clips that
  survive on Kick get channels terminated on YouTube. Never mirror Kick gambling
  content to YouTube.

### Other recurring flags
- Misleading thumbnails/titles (removal); sexualized thumbnails (age-gate +
  demotion) — the IG "poster formula" problem applies to YouTube thumbnails too.
- Reused/watermarked content is ineligible for monetization (relevant to clients
  dumping TikToks onto Shorts).
- Harassment policy covers "prank" content where the target is distressed;
  YouTube additionally bans dangerous pranks outright.

## TikTok

### Enforcement layers
- **Removal + 4-tier ladder:** warning → temporary feature restriction →
  account restriction → ban; TikTok may offer policy "training" to restore
  access. Strikes expire but repeat categories escalate fast.
- **For You feed (FYF) ineligibility** = TikTok's demotion layer and the analog
  of Instagram recommendation ineligibility: content stays up but gets no FYF
  distribution. Categories largely mirror Instagram's: sexually suggestive,
  dangerous stunts, tobacco/alcohol-centric, gambling, borderline graphic,
  engagement bait/spam.
- **"Content under review"** gating silently holds distribution during Trust &
  Safety review — the "my views flatlined for 2 days" complaint.
- Monetizing creators are explicitly held to a higher bar (Creator Rewards:
  10K followers + 100K views/30d).

### Branded content & disclosure (actively enforced)
- TikTok auto-detects undisclosed branded content within hours of posting;
  undisclosed commercial content gets visibility reduction and/or forced
  disclosure toggling; repeat failures → posting restrictions or ban. If the
  client does sponsor deals anywhere, TikTok is where non-disclosure gets caught
  first.

### Gambling
- Organic gambling promotion and referral codes are prohibited; casino-content
  accounts live under permanent FYF-ineligibility risk even when not promoting.
