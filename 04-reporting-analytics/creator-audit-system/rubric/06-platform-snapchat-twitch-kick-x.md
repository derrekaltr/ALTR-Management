# Rubric 06 — Cross-Platform: Snapchat (+ funnel context: Twitch, Kick, X)
(Grounded Oct 2026. AUDIT SCOPE: Snapchat only. The Twitch/Kick/X sections are
FUNNEL CONTEXT — used to assess links found in a client's chain, never to
produce a platform grade or audit section.)

## Snapchat

- **Two-tier reality:** Community Guidelines apply account-wide, but **Spotlight
  (the monetized/discovery surface) is held to an "appropriate for all
  audiences" bar** — stricter than Instagram's recommendation layer. Suggestive
  content, gambling, tobacco/alcohol focus, and risky stunts are Spotlight-dead
  even when allowed on the account.
- **Monetization (2026):** verified identity required; FTC-aligned disclosure
  required on sponsored Spotlight content — sustained disclosure failures are a
  deplatforming offense, not just a demotion.
- **Fully AI-generated videos are ineligible for Spotlight rewards** (policy
  effective Jul 31, 2026) — relevant to clients running AI-slideshow side
  accounts.
- Public Profiles/Stories have their own discover-eligibility moderation;
  the "personal vs. public" split matters — flags on public content hit the
  whole profile's distribution.

## Twitch

- **Unified Sexual Content Policy** + Content Classification Labels (CCL):
  mislabeling a stream is itself enforceable; suggestive content requires the
  label and is excluded from the front page.
- **Gambling policy:** streaming unlicensed slots/roulette/dice sites (the
  Stake.com class) is banned; **sponsorships by banned sites are banned too**,
  including logos/codes on stream. Licensed-operator content is allowed but
  ad-limited. Circumvention (mirroring, link workarounds) is called out
  explicitly and draws harsher action.
- **Off-platform conduct policy** (expanded late 2025): serious behavior on
  other platforms can cost the Twitch channel — a client's Instagram/Kick
  conduct is Twitch-relevant.
- Enforcement: warnings → suspensions → indefinite bans; DMCA strikes run on a
  separate track (3 = termination).

## Kick

- The permissive platform — gambling streams are core to its business — but not
  rule-free: NSFW requires 18+ tags, and **since Feb 2026 only approved gambling
  sites (with KYC/identity verification) may be streamed**; promoting
  unapproved sites is enforceable even on Kick.
- Strategic role in our audits: Kick is the correct OFF-RAMP for gambling
  monetization that must come off Instagram/YouTube — but it must stay
  one-directional. Kick content, codes, and sponsor marks must never flow back
  to the strict platforms (the #1 cross-contamination mistake).
- Brand-safety note for clients: mainstream sponsors discount Kick-first
  creators; the Kick money and the brand-deal money usually can't both be
  maximized.

## X (Twitter)

- Adult content is permitted **with media labeling**; unlabeled sensitive media
  is the enforceable offense. Gambling promotion is looser organically but paid
  promotion is regulated.
- Role in audits: X is where adult-adjacent creators can legitimately route the
  spicy funnel instead of Instagram — flag it as the compliant alternative, and
  check the client's X isn't itself cross-linking back to IG in bios that
  Instagram resolves.

## Cross-platform audit principles

1. **Score each platform against its own system** — a grid that's fine on X can
   be fatal on YouTube; gambling that's legal on Kick is termination on YouTube.
2. **The funnel map is the finding:** what matters most is which platforms LINK
   to which. Strict platforms must never point at (or carry marks of) content
   that violates them, even when it lives happily elsewhere.
3. **One compliance home per content type:** gambling → Kick (approved sites
   only); adult-adjacent → X/paid platforms; everything else earns its reach on
   the strict platforms. The client's landing page should reflect that routing.
4. **Cross-platform enforcement is real:** Twitch punishes off-platform conduct;
   YouTube terminations echo into brand deals; platforms share scam-pattern
   signals informally via press cycles. One platform's scandal is every
   platform's risk.
