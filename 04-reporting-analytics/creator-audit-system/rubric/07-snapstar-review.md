# Rubric 07 — Snapchat: Snap Star Review Grading
(Grounded Oct 2026. Snap keeps formal criteria private; this rubric reflects the
observable bar from accepted creators + Snap's published monetization terms.
Use alongside rubric 06's Snapchat section.)

## What the Snap Star review actually evaluates

Grade the client's Snapchat on the five axes Snap's internal review weighs.
Each axis scores PASS / AT-RISK / FAIL; the Snapchat letter grade rolls up from
these plus any policy findings.

1. **Scale** — ~50K+ subscribers is the observed floor for Star acceptance;
   monetization program separately requires 50K followers + 15,000 view-hours
   in 28 days with ≥3,000 hours from Spotlight.
2. **Consistency** — daily or near-daily Public Stories; 25+ public
   Stories/Spotlight uploads per month, sustained for months (bursts don't
   count). This is the most common silent failure: clients treat Snap as a
   dumping ground and post in spurts.
3. **Identity** — real name or established brand identity, coherent recognizable
   theme/category. Anonymous meme/repost feeds don't get Starred.
4. **Originality** — original content; reposted TikToks with watermarks and
   meme-only feeds are disqualifying. (Fully AI-generated video is already
   ineligible for Spotlight rewards — rubric 06.)
5. **Advertiser-cleanliness** — the Star review applies a stricter-than-
   guidelines lens: no suggestive-leaning content, gambling, substances,
   harassment-pattern pranks, or misinformation-adjacent content anywhere on
   the public surface. Content that merely gets demoted on Instagram can sink
   a Star application outright. 18+, eligible country.

## How to grade it in an audit

- **Collectable from outside:** public profile existence
  (snapchat.com/add/<handle>), subscriber count if displayed, Spotlight posts
  visible on the web profile, content theme/cleanliness of whatever is public.
- **Must come from the client** (put in the asks list): Stories-per-month
  cadence, Spotlight view-hours (28d), subscriber count if hidden, any past
  Star application or rejection, Account/profile strikes.
- **Grade meaning on the matrix:** the Snapchat grade = "Snap Star readiness,"
  not just policy risk. An account with zero violations but 3 Stories a month
  grades C at best — consistency IS the criterion.

## Standard Snap recommendations by client type

- **Clean lifestyle/comedy creators:** usually the highest-ROI untapped
  platform — Snap monetization pays meaningfully for exactly the
  advertiser-clean content the strict platforms reward. Prescribe: 1 public
  Story/day + 5–8 Spotlight clips/week (vertical recuts of existing IG/TikTok
  content WITHOUT watermarks), real-name public profile, 90-day consistency
  runway before applying.
- **Adult-adjacent/suggestive accounts:** Spotlight's all-audiences bar makes
  Snap the wrong growth bet for the spicy format; only the cleaned-up version
  belongs there.
- **Gambling/crypto accounts:** do not route to Snap at all; the Star review
  is a hard fail and the account history can't be hidden.
