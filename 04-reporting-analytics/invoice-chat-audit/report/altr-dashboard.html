The engine works

The 30% bundled retainer sold to 14 / 14 clients, and the fan-side monetization machine inside it is prolific — a single $50 tape drop did $10k in day one. DMCA takedowns (up to 5,871 removals/client) are the most loved deliverable in every chat.

Discrete upsells barely close

In 13 months, only 3 stand-alone paid add-ons ever converted. Every offer that shipped without a price — Copyright Ban Protection, the Meta-rep cost-split — closed at zero. The one client who asked what the cost-split would cost got no answer.

Clients pay; rails don't

No client is a demonstrated non-payer. ~250–300 avoidable cycle-days of collection lag were ~80% agency-caused: bank churn, invoice errors, link latency, and no receivables reconciliation.

Method & caveats. Built from full reads of all 14 exported chats (parse → extract → cycle-reconstruction pipeline, then per-chat analyst passes; see report/invoicing-audit.md and report/services-catalog.md for message-ID citations).
