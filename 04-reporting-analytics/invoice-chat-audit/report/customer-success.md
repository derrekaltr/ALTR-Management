## 2. Structure of the group chats

**Naming & creation.** `<Model> x ALTR`, created by **Eric The Efficient** as a Telegram supergroup with a branded photo. Client joins by invite link. Eric posts the welcome and go-live target, then goes quiet.

**Standing roster in every group:** Derrek (owner), Eric (setup), Mayson Chat (account manager/technical), Mica Vecco (Social Media Director), Michelle Queue (Promo + DMCA), Stephski (Invoicing), plus the assigned OF/chat lead (Fred | OFM or Ollie Mckinlay). Since Jul 29, 2026: Vivek "@fonta" (Meta Support Rep) in all groups. Larger accounts also carry an AM pair over the chat team (Nate + Ronnie) and occasionally chat managers. **Chatters are never in the client group.** Lauchlann, Sofia and Sofia D'Ortone are seeded into several groups and never speak.

**The five topics** (Telegram forum topics — the whole CS model depends on them):

| Topic | Owner | What belongs there |
|---|---|---|
| **OnlyFans** | Chat lead | Intake form, login/handover, go-live, pricing & limits, custom requests, scripts, chatter issues, vault uploads, Snapchat adds, video calls, whale handling |
| **Social Media** | Mica | Intro + Calendly, handle intake, weekly reel inspo, link-in-bio/.vip domains (Mayson posts here or in general), IG suspension policy, Meta-rep escalations |
| **Promo** | Michelle | IG Reels Marketing PDF + Drive, reel-upload chases, story stills, managed-IG handle disclosure, Derrek's promo-infrastructure broadcasts |
| **Invoicing** | Stephski | Intro at first cycle, withdrawal reminders, invoice PDFs, payment chasing, rail changes, FX |
| **DMCA** | Michelle | Weekly leak-removal screenshot; client-submitted leak/impersonation links |

**General (no-topic) thread** is where things go wrong: it carries welcome messages, Mayson's routing pings, and — on the worst accounts — most of the conversation (Sarah Lloyd 76%, Kaitlynn 71%, Vicky 66%, Amber 48%, Haileigh 43%, Lauryn 41%). Sarah Lloyd's group has **no OnlyFans topic at all**, so all chat-team issues run in general. Vicky's Invoicing topic was created 14 days late. The clean accounts are the ones where the lead enforces topics (Lilia 12%, Shayna 7%, Mila 4%).

**Pinned messages** are the only in-chat "system of record": the Drive quick-access index, the customs sheet, and (older accounts) the team roster. Most groups have nothing pinned.

**Routing convention.** Mayson (and sometimes the client) @-mentions the owner with "^^" (e.g. `@olkmck ^^`, `@ofmfrederick ^^`). It works when the tagged person is online; it is also the most common way a question dies.

## 3. What gets sent in each topic — the standard messages

### OnlyFans topic (chat lead)

1. **Lead intro** — Ollie: *"Hey Lauren, I'm Ollie nice to meet you, will be handling your chatting."* Fred/Mayson use the longer canned version: *"My names X, I handle all things OnlyFans so if you've ever got any questions then pleeeeease do not hesitate to ask!!! I'm always online so I will get a response to you promptly... ALWAYS!!!"*
2. **Intake form ask** — *"please fill out the form that will give the team everything they need to know about yourself AND the ability to sell in confidence. Making sure that nothing you DO NOT want sold is ever sold"* + link (Google Form / Google Doc / Typeform depending on lead).
3. **Go-live** — *"we are starting today at 2pm EST. Was just wondering about snapchat adds? Customs? and Videocalls? If you can let me know pricing"* → *"All in — team will begin now!"*
4. **Pricing probes** — *"what's the minimum price for a custom?"*, *"are you adding on snapchat and if so how much?"*
5. **Custom request block** — `—— CUSTOM REQUEST —— Model / Fan / Paid / Duration / Details` (screenshot of the fan's message attached); detailed customs go in a Google Doc; from Aug 2026 the pinned **customs Google Sheet** (*"here is your custom sheet"*).
6. **Snapchat add** — *"he paid for a snapchat add… to be added on snap"* with the fan's username; model adds manually.
7. **FAQ re-collection** (on team swaps) — *"could you fill in this FAQ for us as well please so as a chat team we can have more information on you 🙏🏼"*
8. **Setup complete** (Mayson, day 3–17) — *"Just a quick update, we've finished setting up and optimizing your profile. Tracking links are added, promo campaigns for both new and expired subscribers are now running 24/7, automated renewal messages… win-back messages… bio/settings have been optimized."*
9. **Chatter-issue acknowledgements** — *"Relaying to the team - will add this to the vault notes as well!"*, *"I have passed this on and will be in contact with chatters."*

### Social Media topic (Mica; Mayson for links)

1. **Intro** — *"Hi! I'm Mica, your Social Media Director 💻💕 I'll be helping you grow your social media pages… IG in addition to TikTok, Snapchat, FB… my booking link to schedule a 1 on 1 call"* + Calendly (30 min).

## 6. What works

- **Topic discipline, when enforced by the lead.** Lilia/Shayna/Mila run at 4–12% off-topic traffic and questions get answered because the owner sees them. Fred's *"just responding here so we can keep the conversations on OnlyFans all in place"* is the model.
- **Scripted intros with an artifact attached.** Every department's intro ends in a form, a booking link, or a PDF+Drive. Clients complete intake forms in 20–45 minutes when asked on day 0.
- **Mayson's setup-complete milestone.** A concrete "you are live and optimized" moment clients respond well to.
- **The customs Google Sheet.** Pinned, real-time, replaces the request-block sprawl; rolled out to 5 accounts in three weeks.
- **Stephski's cadence.** Withdrawal reminder → invoice → +3-day nudge → receipt, biweekly, every account. Predictable and polite; clients pay reliably (see the invoicing audit).
- **Weekly reel inspo + weekly DMCA report.** Two cheap, visible touches per week on every account regardless of anything else happening.
- **Vivek's arrival.** Within 10 days of being added he recovered accounts for Sarah H, gave a FB status to Sarah Lloyd, and took Barbara's Copyright Ban Protection request — the first time IG issues had a named owner inside the chat.
- **Chat leads who front-run problems.** Ollie's *"just logged into your OF from my phone"* pre-empting the security email; Fred clearing the auto-scheduler queue and posting the screenshot.
- **The go-live conventions.** Scheduled hard start, both pages simultaneously, billing from go-live date — no client has disputed a start date.
- **Fast median response.** Typical replies land within the hour on most accounts.

## 7. Systemic CS gaps (ranked by frequency × impact)

1. **No enforcement loop from intake form → chatters.** The #1 complaint on 8 of 14 accounts: chatters selling prohibited content, wrong prices, pre-cutoff content, or implying acts the model doesn't do — after she wrote it down. Clients end up policing chat themselves ("makes me feel lowkey crazy having to ask the same thing every day").
2. **The "^^" routing dead-ends.** Mayson's tag-and-move-on works only if the tagged lead is online; several of the never-answered threads in §1 end at a "^^". No escalation timer exists.
3. **Questions asked in the general thread get lost.** Six accounts run 40–76% of traffic outside topics; Sarah Lloyd has no OnlyFans topic at all.
4. **Team swaps re-onboard the client at her expense.** Fred → Ollie on Vale and Vicky: Drive deleted, April customs never delivered, 7 Infloww relinks, FAQ redone, logins drifting between leads (Lilia gave new logins to Mayson; Fred didn't know). No handover checklist exists.
5. **No named account owner on several accounts.** Lauryn has had no chat lead in the OF topic since June; Barbara's chat side is invisible; Haileigh asked "Do I have an account manager?" in March. Where Derrek named a POC (Barbara/Mayson) the client still asked "any updates?" and got "Yo Ma'am".
6. **Promises without a delivery tracker.** Vicky's traffic ("end of week one" → 7 weeks later "next 48 hrs"); Amber's IG accounts (3 built, 3 banned, disclosed only when asked; 35 followers months later); Lauren Blake's ads reporting; Barbara's backup IG. The Aug 1 memo paused promo across the board with "updates next week."
7. **IG questions from clients go unanswered unless Vivek picks them up.** Mila's "my account's been suspended again… so what happens now" (Jun 23) was never answered; Haileigh's shadowban ask got "Sure share login" and stopped.
8. **Credential handling has no system.** Passwords in chat (sometimes at staff request), pre-group DMs to Derrek that nobody else can see, no password manager, 2FA relinks on every change.
9. **Assets scattered across personal accounts and devices.** Drives on individual staff Google accounts, shared albums on personal phones, intake responses in the lead's form account, credentials in DMs. Anyone new to the account starts blind ("Ah I wasn't on here when it was sent").
10. **Reporting to clients is gone.** The 2025 accounts got a report PDF + 14-day plan every two weeks; 2026 accounts get nothing except invoices and DMCA screenshots. Clients ask for stats (Amber, Lauren Blake) and get a phone call promise.
11. **Chatter quality / voice mismatch** across at least 7 accounts: broken English, emoticons the model hates, "are you single" to whales, aggressive tone, telling fans she has a husband. Remedies are reactive (pay deductions, removing a chatter).
12. **Whale retention after takeover is unmeasured.** Lauren Blake: 13 of 24 whales (59% of revenue) ghosted in the first month; Lilia lost her top spender to a missed flag; Amber's top spender "picked up on" the tone change.
13. **Money-side friction lands in the CS chat** (documented in the invoicing audit): four rail changes in 2026, Zelle caps forcing 5×$500 sends, fraud-flagged wires, FX reissues, a premature invoice, off-book payments to Derrek. Stephski absorbs it well, but each event costs client trust.
14. **DMCA is opaque.** Clients receive a screenshot with a number; the first one arrives unexplained ("What's this?"); high-leak clients say the volume is overwhelming and submit their own links.
