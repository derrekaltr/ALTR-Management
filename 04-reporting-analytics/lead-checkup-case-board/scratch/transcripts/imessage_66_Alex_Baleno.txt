[2026-04-26 23:55] Alex Baleno: WhatsApp is a messaging app, not a browser. You cannot embed a full webpage, a multi-screen dashboard, or a persistent UI panel inside a chat thread. What you can do, using the WhatsApp Business API, is send structured interactive messages. There are a few real building blocks:
List messages — a button that opens a menu of up to 10 options. Customer taps "Browse vehicles," gets a list: "Supercars / Luxury Sedans / SUVs / Convertibles." Taps one, you send the next list.
