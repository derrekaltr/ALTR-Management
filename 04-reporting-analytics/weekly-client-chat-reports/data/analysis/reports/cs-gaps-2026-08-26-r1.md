## The gaps, ranked

### 1. Nobody owns the account once onboarding ends  *(root cause — named as the #1 fix in 15 of 19 analyses)*
Sophie (Raw Agency) acted as the de-facto account manager in Sep–Oct 2025: weekly numbers, written plans, check-ins. When she left, **no one inherited the role on any account.** Since then, requests are routed by @-tagging whoever seems relevant.
- Haileigh Cox, 3/22: *"Who do I reach out to for general messages here. I feel like every time i reach out it's either super delayed or just no response at all."* — and later: *"Do I have an account manager?"*
- Barbara, 7/26: *"if i have any questions regarding anything who would be the right person to reach out to from the group?"*
- Sarah Lloyd tags two AMs in 30+ messages; Mayson's `@natehei ^^` relay appears 14× and reliably predicts a dropped request.
- Shayna: every OF item routes through Fred; Mayson's role was to tag him (11×). Shayna, 8/11: *"Can someone answer in the chat all my messages are just left on seen 🥲"*
- Sarah's IG ownership rotated Sophie → Mica → Michelle → Derrek → Vivek. Lauryn's Facebook issue had **no owner for ~9 months** (open 334 days).
- Vivek, 8/12: *"Just waiting on derrek / Once he wakes up"* — the owner is the bottleneck for escalations.

### 2. Promises lapse silently — 47% of everything we say we'll do
Nothing tracks commitments, so a lapse only surfaces when the client chases — and several have stopped chasing.

- Liza: paid-ads stats requested 2025-11-18 (*"Is there any statistics I can see?"*) — never delivered, 9 months.
- Lauryn: FB monetisation *"within a month"* — never. Bi-weekly report + 14-day plan — sent once.
- Vale: six scheduling promises for paid calls lapsed May→Aug; John's $1,800 call logged May 1 still unbooked.
- Lauren Blake: Stephski 8/3 *"Derreks going to give you a ring and walk you through your current ad stats"* — 23 days, no call.

- Amber Haze: Derrek 5/20 *"I'm gonna add a few thousand dollars to pay Ads to finish the month strong"* — never evidenced. 14 of 24 promises lapsed.

## What works — keep doing it (clients told us in their own words)
- **A written numbers update.** Sophie's week-1 reports drew the warmest reactions on every account (*"Wohooo thannk you so much!"*, *"Your chatting is INSANE!!"*, Lauryn *"I think we are on the right track"*). It has not been done since.
- **Michelle's weekly leak-removal report** — the one reliable cadence; clients thank her explicitly (Shayna *"Thank you for being so on top of this"*, Lilia *"U guys are seriously the best!"*).
- **Long, honest explanations.** Derrek's Aug 1 phone-farm message bought goodwill instantly on every chat (*"awesome thanks Derrek :))) appreciate you a bunch"*). The failure was only the follow-up.
- **Fast, hands-on fixes with a visible owner** — Mica's Nov 2025 unban with daily updates; Vivek's same-hour IG fixes for Paige; Nick's 48h FB impersonator takedown (*"Amazing thank you Nick great job"*).
- **Named-fan, named-number collaboration** — Ollie flagging Joe at $6.3k, Lauren Blake delivering in 40 min → *"Bravo team!! That's what we love to see!!"*; Ollie's day-3 revenue proof to Vale earned pricing trust.
- **Chatters asking before selling** (King Choy on Lauryn: *"I just want to make sure we set the expectations before we take on some requests"*) and visible aftercare on big spenders (Vladan).

## Fix set (in priority order)
1. **One named account owner per client, announced in the chat**, with a named backup, a 24h acknowledgement rule, and ownership of a visible open-items list. (Gaps 1, 6, 8)
2. **A written Friday update, unprompted, every account:** revenue vs last week, what shipped (with proof/links), IG status in plain words, open items with owner + date, payment status. *(The reporting pipeline can draft this automatically from the chat data each week.)* (Gaps 2, 3, 5, 6)
3. **Promise ledger:** every "I'll get back to you" gets an owner and date and is closed in-thread in writing. (Gap 2)
4. **Billing stability:** one rail, no more bank changes without 30 days' notice; receipt confirmed within 24h; pre-send invoice checklist (fee %, credits, currency, due date, prior corrections); side-channel payments logged same day; invoices timed to OF payout cycles. (Gap 4)
5. **Platform desk:** Vivek owns a visible ticket list per client with a twice-weekly status, and always explains the root cause. (Gap 5)
6. **Per-account chatter rulebook, enforced mechanically:** excluded-sub/VIP list checked before every mass message, price floors, hard content lines, no sale without a client brief, alert-the-client rule on big spenders, written confirmation of every client instruction within 2h. (Gap 7)
7. **Onboarding checklist with day-7 and day-30 reviews:** intake form reviewed live with the client, kickoff booked, "who to contact for what" pinned, schedule dates confirmed same day. (Gap 8)
8. **Security:** never request or post credentials in a group — DM or password manager only; rotate every currently exposed credential this week. (Gap 9)
9. **Quiet-client rule:** any client silent 7+ days, or any complaint, gets a human non-invoice check-in within 48h; any exit message gets an owner reply and a call offer within the hour. (Gap 6)
